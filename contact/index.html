Frequently asked questions

What does Platino Cube do?

We are a marketing, software and AI agency. We handle social media, content, branding, events, websites and software for businesses.

Who do you work with?

Mostly B2B companies, but we also work with clinics, startups and other businesses that want to look professional.

Can you handle everything, or only one service?

Both. Start with one service, or let us manage your brand, pages, website and events together.

How do prices work?

Every business is different, so we give a custom quote for each project or monthly plan, after we understand your needs.

How do I get a quote?

Send the form or message us on WhatsApp. We will ask a few questions and send you a proposal. We reply on WhatsApp very fast, and by email within 24 hours.

How do you use AI?

We use AI to plan, draft and speed up production, and our team always reviews and finishes the work. You get faster delivery without losing the human touch.

Can my website and content be in Arabic and English?

Yes, we build and write in both languages.

Do you work outside Amman?

We are based in Amman and focus on Jordan right now.

What do I need to start?

Just your idea and your goals. We guide you from there.

Do you create video and UGC content?

Yes. We produce videos, reels, images, designs and UGC-style content for your social media and ads.

Do you build software?

We are building custom software and AI tools. Tell us what you need and we will tell you what is possible.
