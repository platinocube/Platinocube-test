Our story

Platino Cube started with the AI era. We saw that businesses wanted a strong, professional presence on social media, but running it was slow, expensive and complicated. We also saw that AI could change that, if it is guided by people who truly understand marketing.

So we built a team that does both. We bring more than nine years of experience in marketing, social media strategy and event planning, and we pair it with AI tools that make our work faster, sharper and easier for our clients to follow.

We start every project by getting to know the client: what they sell, who they sell to, and what makes their work easier. Then we make marketing that fits.
